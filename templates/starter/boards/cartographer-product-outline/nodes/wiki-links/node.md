Never stored — recomputed from the note bodies on every read, so they cannot go stale. Renaming a node rewrites every link pointing at it.

A title nothing answers to becomes a **ghost**: a dashed dot you can click to bring into being. And since v1.8 a title no *node* claims resolves to a board instead, rendered with ↗ — nodes are always asked first, so no old note changes meaning.
