---

Agent-created nodes carry a bot glyph naming the token and the date. `createdVia` is the server's to set: the canvas round-trips it and the save path ignores the client's copy — a mark you could quietly remove is not attribution.

An agent's first write to a board snapshots it first, best-effort — a safety net that can refuse the work it protects is the worse failure mode.
