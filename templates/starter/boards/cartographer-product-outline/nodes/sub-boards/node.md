title: Sub-boards
created: '2026-07-31'
status: documented
tags: []
description: Any node can drill into a board of its own.
---

This is how a map stays readable as it grows: the overview stays an overview, and the detail lives one level down. Breadcrumbs walk back up. A frame promotes into one in a single operation, taking its members and their internal edges and leaving a drill-down node behind.

A child board can be a graph — which is why the word is sub-board, not sub-map.
