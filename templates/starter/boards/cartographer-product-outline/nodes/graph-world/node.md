Dots sized by connectedness; d3-force places them. Use it when what matters is how things relate rather than what order they happen in.

Drawn edges carry the same arrowhead as the map's. The fainter, arrowless layer is drawn straight from your prose — see [[Wiki-links]]: a mention notices something, a drawn edge points somewhere.
