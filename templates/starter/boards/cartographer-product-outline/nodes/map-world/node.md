Cards with shape, colour and status; chips for smaller things. Edges carry labels and an arrowhead. Colour is not decoration here — it is how a map says two things are the same kind of thing without drawing an edge between them.

A Move tool on `H` joins Select on `V`, because every other pan gesture is invisible to someone holding an ordinary mouse.
