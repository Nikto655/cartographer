id: archive-never-delete
title: 'Archive, never delete'
created: '2026-07-31'
status: documented
tags: []
description: Every destructive action moves files under `archive/`.
---

Recovery is one `mv`. An archived board takes its snapshots and archived nodes with it, so a later board reusing the slug cannot inherit them. There is no delete anywhere in the product — archive is what deletion *is* here.
