Recent answers "what was I doing" and pins starred boards above the grid; All shows the tree; Folders shows the arrangement. Sort and multi-select work in all three — the dashboard *opens* on Recent, so a control missing there is a control the app appears not to have.

Looking at a board is not changing it: a pan or a zoom no longer writes the board back, so recency describes work, not attention.
